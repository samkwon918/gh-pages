# Carity Prime Days Campaign — "The Organic Switch"

**Prepared by:** West (Roles 7, 3, 4, 2, 5)
**Product:** 50-pad organic cotton multipack — **$19.99** (Prime Day price)
**Window:** 2 weeks pre-Prime Day → Prime Day → 1 week post

---

## The Insight (Role 7)

Prime Day is the one moment a year when **tens of millions of women are actively looking to stock up on household essentials at a discount.** Pads are a Prime Day staple — Always, Kotex, and Tampax run bulk deals and dominate "subscribe & save" pushes. That means millions of women will literally be searching "pads" on Amazon this week.

The opportunity isn't competing on price with Always ($0.22/pad). It's intercepting the **switch moment**: the woman who's been buying synthetic pads by default, who sees an organic option at $0.40/pad during a deals event where she's *already* in buying mode, and thinks "for $19.99, why not try it?"

Prime Day isn't a discount event for Carity. It's a **trial event.**

**The campaign name: "The Organic Switch"**

---

## Campaign Architecture

Three phases. Each phase has a different job.

### Phase 1: PRE-HEAT (Days -14 to -1)

**Job:** Build the audience pool. Create awareness before the noise starts. Seed the "switch" narrative.

### Phase 2: PRIME DAY (Day 0-1)

**Job:** Convert. Every dollar, every impression, every word drives to the listing. Speed and urgency.

### Phase 3: AFTERBURN (Days +1 to +7)

**Job:** Catch the late buyers, convert the considerers, and lock in subscribers.

## Phase 2: PRIME DAY (Day 0-1)

### 2A. Amazon Ads — Full Throttle (Role 4)

**Budget:** Allocate the biggest ad spend of the quarter to these 48 hours. Every dollar delayed to next week is worth less.

**Campaign structure:**

| Campaign | Type | Targeting | Bid Strategy |
|----------|------|-----------|-------------|
| Brand Defense | Sponsored Products | "Carity" branded terms | Low bid, high placement |
| Category Grab | Sponsored Products | "organic pads," "period pads organic," "cotton pads" | Aggressive bid, top-of-search |
| Competitor Conquest | Sponsored Products | Rael, Honest, Cora, L. ASINs | Moderate bid |
| Competitor Keywords | Sponsored Products | "[brand] pads," "[brand] alternative" | Moderate bid |
| Brand Story | Sponsored Brands | Category terms | Video or Store Spotlight format |
| Retarget | Sponsored Display | Viewed Carity but didn't buy | Aggressive bid |

**The killer ad:** Sponsored Brands Video — 15-second video showing:
- Open the box (all 3 sizes visible)
- Hold one pad up (paper-thin)
- Text overlay: "50 organic pads. 3 sizes. $19.99. The Organic Switch."
- No voiceover needed. Clean, fast, product-focused.

### 2B. Lightning Deal or Prime Exclusive (Role 4)

If eligible:
- **Prime Exclusive Discount** (badge on listing) — even 5% off $19.99 = $18.99 with the Prime badge, which signals "deal" in search results
- **Lightning Deal** — short burst visibility on the Deals page. Worth it if Amazon offers a reasonable fee.
- **Coupon** — if no deal eligibility, a clippable coupon ($1-2 off) adds the green badge to the listing

The badge matters more than the discount amount. In Prime Day search results, badged listings get 2-5x the click-through rate of unbadged ones.

## Phase 3: AFTERBURN (Days +1 to +7)

### 3A. Retargeting (Role 4)

- **Sponsored Display retargeting:** Anyone who viewed the Carity listing but didn't purchase — serve ads for 7 days post-Prime Day
- **Amazon DSP (if budget allows):** Retarget off-Amazon (Instagram, web) with "Missed Prime Day? The Organic Switch is still $19.99"

### 3B. Post-Purchase Sequence (Roles 3 + 5)

Every Prime Day buyer gets this sequence (via Amazon's buyer-seller messaging or product insert card):

**Insert Card (in the box):**
Front: "You just made The Organic Switch. Welcome."
Back:
- "Love it? Subscribe & Save so you never run out."
- "Tell a friend: [short URL to listing]"
- "Questions? We're real people: hello@carity.life"
- QR code → carity.life (start building the direct relationship)

**Post-delivery email (if Amazon allows, or via request-a-review):**
- "How was your first Carity experience?"
- Prompt for review (reviews earned during Prime Day spike carry disproportionate weight — Amazon's algorithm rewards recent review velocity)

### 3C. Convert to Subscribe & Save (Role 5)

The entire point of Prime Day for Carity is not one sale — it's acquiring subscribers.

**Subscribe & Save math:**
- One-time buyer: $19.99, once. LTV = $19.99.
- S&S subscriber at monthly: $19.99 × 12 = ~$240/year. LTV = 12-20x.

Prime Day is the cheapest customer acquisition moment of the year. Every buyer who converts to S&S is the flywheel spinning.

**How to push S&S:**
- Make S&S discount meaningful (at least 5%, ideally 10-15% off)
- Listing bullets mention S&S
- Insert card mentions S&S
- Follow-up email mentions S&S
- The pitch: "You just tried organic. Now never go back — Subscribe & Save and we'll send a fresh box every month."

## Why This Is the Smartest Play (Role 7)

Most small brands treat Prime Day as a discount event — slash margins, hope for volume, lose money on acquisition. That's playing Always's game at Always's scale.

Carity's play is different because the product is different:

1. **You're not discounting. $19.99 is already the lowest organic per-pad price.** The Prime Day badge just gives you visibility you can't normally buy.

2. **The comparison table is a weapon.** No other organic brand offers 50 pads, three sizes, at $0.40/pad. On Prime Day, when shoppers are actively comparing deals, that table does the selling.

3. **The real conversion isn't the first box — it's Subscribe & Save.** A woman who tries Carity on Prime Day and subscribes is worth $240/year. Prime Day's job is to get her to try. The product's job is to make her stay. That's the flywheel from [[synthesis/carity-growth-strategy]].

4. **Prime Day solves Carity's biggest problem: trial.** The wiki research identified that the flywheel ignition point is the first experience. The woman who "couldn't afford Rael, tries Carity, and thinks 'this is better than what I used to buy'" becomes an evangelist. Prime Day is the lowest-friction trial moment of the year.

5. **Every Prime Day buyer is a node in the word-of-mouth network.** 200 new customers telling one friend each = 200 potential zero-CAC acquisitions in the months after. That's the second flywheel.

The campaign name — "The Organic Switch" — is the strategy. Prime Day is just the door.
