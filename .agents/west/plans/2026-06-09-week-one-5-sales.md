## The Strategic Call (Role 7)

Five cold sales in 7 days rules out the slow channels. Paid ads need pixel data and a learning phase. SEO and AI search take months. Influencer seeding rarely converts inside a week.

The fastest legitimate cold path for Carity is the one the research already identified as Moat 3: **institutional buyers**. Women's shelters, food/diaper banks, period-supply nonprofits, community health orgs, and summer youth programs have a *job requirement* to source affordable period products. Cold email to them is B2B, legal, and mission-aligned — and one yes can be a multi-box order.

Note: cold-emailing consumers from purchased lists is off the table (deliverability suicide and brand poison for a trust-based brand). Consumer-side cold motion happens in public — founder posts and community threads — as a supporting track.

**Two tracks:**
- **Track A (primary): institutional cold outbound** — 150 contacts over 5 days. Carries the 5-sale commitment.
- **Track B (supporting): founder-led public posts + local Texas touches** — adds 1-3 bonus sales and builds the retarget pool.

### Funnel Math (Role 4)

| Stage | Rate | Count |
|-------|------|-------|
| Cold contacts sent (Track A) | — | 150 |
| Replies (mission-aligned, hyper-relevant offer) | ~10% | 15 |

West's cold-email benchmarks put generic B2B reply rates at 5-10%. We're writing to people whose literal job is finding affordable period products, with a $19.99 ask — top of that range is realistic. 150 contacts = 30/day for 5 days at ~2 min personalization each (~1 hr/day of sending).

## The Offer (Roles 6 + 7)

Make yes trivial. Three rungs:

1. **Pilot Box — $19.99.** One 50-pad multipack (28 regular / 12 large / 10 overnight) for their supply closet. This is the cold CTA. One pilot box = one sale.
2. **Starter Bundle — 5 multipacks (250 pads) at a founder discount.** Offer in conversation, not in email #1. *(Founder sets the price — suggest ~$99-110, roughly 15-20% off; create a hidden Shopify product or discount code `PARTNER15`.)*
3. **Standing order** — pitch the existing 3/6-month subscription plans only after a pilot lands. Not a cold ask.

**Optional sweetener (founder's call):** "Order this week and we match it — every box you buy, we donate a box's worth of pads to a Texas shelter." Cora donates *from a distance*; Carity matching alongside a buyer who serves this population is on-brand. Only promise it if you'll do it.

**Compliance (CAN-SPAM):** physical mailing address in the footer, honest subject lines, working opt-out line ("Reply 'no thanks' and I won't follow up"). B2B outreach to organizational addresses is fine.

## If It's Working / If It Isn't

- **Working (≥3 sales by Friday):** double Saturday's send volume from list overflow; ask buyers for one referral org each ("who else should have this?") — referred orgs convert at multiples of cold.
- **Not working (<2 conversations by Wednesday):** the ask may still be too heavy. Drop to: "Can I mail you one free sample pad pack?" — costs pennies, restarts conversations, converts to paid boxes next week. The week-one number matters less than igniting the institutional channel.
