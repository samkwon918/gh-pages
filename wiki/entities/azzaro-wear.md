## The Teen Streetwear Market — Key Dynamics

**Why teen streetwear is hard and high-upside:**
- Teens don't buy products. They buy *identity*. The brand has to mean something socially — it has to give them a signal to send to their peers.
- Social proof is everything. One right influencer, one viral moment, and the curve goes vertical. One wrong signal and the brand feels dead.
- The purchase decision often happens online but the *conversation* that drives it happens at school, on social media, in group chats.
- Price sensitivity is real but brand power overcomes it. Teens will pay $60 for a tee they want and reject a $15 one they don't feel.

**The summer angle:**
- Summer = freedom, energy, outdoor life, identity construction away from school
- Teen boys in summer are image-conscious in a way they won't admit but act on constantly
- Summer drops, limited runs, and seasonal energy create urgency that sustains attention

## The Streetwear Playbook (applied)

1. **Drops, not catalogs.** Streetwear lives on scarcity. A drop (limited quantity, announced in advance, sells out) creates urgency and social signal. A catalog is a commodity.
2. **Community before product.** The brand that has a crew before it has a product wins. Who are the first 100 customers? Make them feel like founders.
3. **Graphic tees as communication.** Every graphic is a statement the wearer is making. What statements does Azzaro Wear make? They need to be statements teen boys *want* to make.
4. **TikTok and Instagram are the store.** The website is the checkout. Discovery happens on social. The brand has to live there first.
5. **Athletes, artists, and local legends.** Not macro-celebrities. The kid at school who everyone watches — that's the influencer. Micro is more credible.

## Moat-Building for Azzaro Wear

- **Brand identity** — what does Azzaro Wear *mean*? What crew does wearing it put you in?
- **Community** — a Discord, a group chat, a comment section that feels like a club
- **Design edge** — graphics that are genuinely cool, not derivative. The design IS the product.
