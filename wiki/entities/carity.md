## The Carity Flywheel (draft — to be refined)

Based on DTC e-commerce principles and the Amazon model, Carity's growth engine should compound like this:

```
Obsessive Product Quality + Experience
          ↓
Delighted First-Time Customer
          ↓
Word of Mouth + Repeat Purchase
          ↓
Lower Blended CAC / Higher LTV
          ↓
Reinvest in Product + Brand
          ↓
Even Better Quality + Experience
```

The element that starts this flywheel: **the first experience must be so good the customer tells someone.** Every other investment flows from that.

## Moat-Building Priorities (draft)

Based on what's known, Carity's moats to build toward:

1. **Brand moat** — a name and identity that customers connect with emotionally, not just transactionally
2. **Retention moat** — subscriptions, loyalty, personalization that makes switching feel wrong
3. **Customer data moat** — proprietary knowledge of who buys, when they rebuy, what they say, what they need next
4. **Community moat** — customers who identify with each other *through* Carity
