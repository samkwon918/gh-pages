# Carity Week-One Sales Sprint

The strategic decision and funnel design behind the June 9–16 sprint to land 5 cold sales — the first live activation of the institutional channel identified as Moat 3 in [[synthesis/carity-growth-strategy]].

## The Decision

Five cold sales in seven days eliminates the slow channels: paid ads (no pixel data, learning phase), SEO/AI search (months), influencer seeding (unreliable inside a week). The fastest legitimate cold path is **institutional cold outbound** — women's shelters, food/diaper banks, period-supply nonprofits, and summer youth programs whose job is sourcing exactly what Carity sells (→ [[concepts/period-poverty]] institutional opportunity).

Cold-emailing consumers from lists was ruled out: deliverability damage and brand poison for a trust-based brand. Consumer cold motion happens in public (founder posts) as a supporting track only.

## The Funnel

150 institutional contacts → ~10% reply (mission-aligned, hyper-relevant) → ~⅓ of conversations convert → **5 pilot-box sales**. The cold CTA is deliberately trivial: one $24.99 multipack for their supply closet, with a 5-pack bundle and standing-order subscription held back for conversations.

## Seasonal Correction

Mid-June means the school-nurse channel (from [[entities/carity]] secondary target) is dormant until August. Active-in-summer substitutes: shelters, food banks, Alliance for Period Supplies affiliates, YMCAs/summer programs, church women's ministries. Texas metros first — the Texas organic cotton story lands hardest at home. School contacts get a "plant the seed for fall" variant but don't count toward the 5.

## New Intelligence Gathered

- **carity.life confirmed live** (Shopify): tagline "Care For All," $24.99 price point, 1/3/6-month subscriptions plus a 12-month "Diamond Plan," contact page already fielding donation inquiries.
- **The site returns 403 to non-browser agents.** Google has it indexed, but AI-search crawlers (ChatGPT, Perplexity class) are almost certainly blocked — meaning zero AI-search citations while that firewall stays as-is. Flagged as a fix; not blocking this week's goal.
